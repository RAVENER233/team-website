Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about DynamoRaven, our team and the projects we build.

Why did we choose the name DynamoRaven?

We chose DynamoRaven to represent creativity, energy and teamwork as we continue learning and building projects together.

What is the main goal of our team?

Our goal is to improve our technical skills, solve practical problems and gain experience by developing useful web applications.

How do we work together on projects?

We divide tasks between team members, share ideas and use GitHub to manage our code and collaborate on development.

What are we planning to learn next?

We plan to explore more advanced JavaScript, backend development and other technologies that will help us build better applications.
